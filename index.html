What we do

Services

If it has wheels and it is broke, call us and describe it. If it is not something we take on, we will tell you straight and point you somewhere that does.

Brakes & Suspension

Stopping and riding right

Pads, rotors, calipers, and brake lines. Shocks, struts, ball joints, leaf springs, and lift and leveling work on trucks.

Engine

Diagnostics and repair

Check engine lights, no-starts, misfires, overheating, belts, hoses, water pumps, and tune-ups.

Driveline

Transmission and axles

U-joints, CV axles, wheel bearings, clutches, differentials, and transfer cases. Front and rear end noises tracked down.

Maintenance

Keeping it on the road

Oil and filter changes, fluid services, batteries, alternators, starters, tires, and pre-trip checkovers.

Powersports

ATV, UTV, and side-by-side

Full suspension and driveline work on Can-Am, Polaris, Arctic Cat, and more. Belts, clutches, A-arms, and axles.

Custom

Motorcycles and builds

Bike service and repair, plus custom and bobber builds. Fabrication, wiring, and one-off work by arrangement.
